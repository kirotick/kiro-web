Shop owners: delete the whole shop account

Staff: delete your own login

Ask your shop owner to switch off your login, or email [privacy email address] with the shop name and your login ID. We delete it after checking with the shop.

What is deleted, and when

Deleted within 30 daysKept, and why
Logins and password hashes, staff details, menu and stock, sales and reports, customer names and phones, Ask KIRO settings Invoice records, only if the shop asks us to keep them for it (GST law requires the shop to keep invoices for 72 months). Nothing else.

Signing out of the app or uninstalling it removes the sign-in token from your phone, but does not delete your account. POS data saved in a browser stays on that device until it is cleared there (More, then Data, then Reset).
