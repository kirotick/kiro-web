Privacy policy

Last updated 5 November 2026

This policy covers the KIRO website, the KIRO POS app (in the browser) and the KIRO Admin app for Android and iPhone. KIRO is run by [Legal name of the business], [Registered address] ("KIRO", "we"). Questions: [privacy email address].

KIRO is software for shops and restaurants. The shop decides what it records about its own customers and staff; for that data, the shop is responsible and KIRO handles it on the shop's behalf.

The KIRO Admin app has no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools, and does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos. We do not sell data or share it for advertising.

Payments

KIRO does not take or hold money. A UPI payment goes straight from the customer's UPI app to the shop's bank account. KIRO cannot see bank accounts, so a UPI payment in KIRO is marked "confirmed by cashier, not verified" until the shop checks it against its bank statement.

Who else handles data

  • Neon: hosts the KIRO server database (Singapore).
  • Netlify: serves this website and the POS app files.
  • An AI provider for Ask KIRO, only if it is switched on, and only the totals and item names described above.
  • Authorities, when Indian law requires us to.

How long we keep it

Business data is kept while the shop's KIRO account is active. When an account is deleted, we delete its data within 30 days. Invoice records are the one exception: Indian GST law requires the shop to keep invoices (currently 72 months from the due date of the annual return, CGST Act section 36), so we give the shop an export first, and keep invoice records only if the shop asks us to keep them for it. Data on the shop's own device stays there until the shop clears it.

Your choices

  • See, correct or delete your data: email [privacy email address]. Staff and customers of a shop can also ask the shop directly.

  • Complaints: write to [privacy email address]. We reply within 7 working days.

Security

Connections use HTTPS. Passwords are stored only as hashes, logins lock after repeated wrong passwords, and each shop can see only its own data. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting your data, we will tell you and the authorities as Indian law requires. To report a security problem, see security.txt.

Children

KIRO is for businesses and is not meant for anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date above. Big changes are also announced in the apps.
